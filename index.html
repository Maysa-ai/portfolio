How I work

I’m at my best when the path isn’t obvious yet — and the team needs to find a useful next step together.

01 / GET CLEAR

Start with the real problem

I ask what people are trying to do, what the business needs, and what we actually know. Research and evidence help separate the urgent from the important.

02 / ALIGN

Make the work shared

I bring product, design, engineering and business perspectives into the same conversation. A clear problem gives people room to disagree constructively and commit.

03 / LEARN BY DOING

Move, measure, adjust

I prefer a useful test over a perfect plan. Ship something small, learn from how it performs, and scale what earns its place — technology included.
